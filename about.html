how vent. works
a place to say whatever's on your mind. no filters on your feelings — just two rules, and a few quiet systems working in the background.

community rules

  • no 18+ / explicit content. sexual or explicit material isn't allowed.
  • no bullying. targeting or insulting another person isn't allowed. venting about your own experiences — even painful ones, including suicide or self-harm — is never a violation and never gets punished.

how moderation works

  • bullying: a flagged post gets an automatic reply telling the user to stop, before anything else happens.
  • name detector: if a post targets someone by typing their username, that person simply won't see it — it shows as an empty post to them, shielding them automatically.
  • suicide / self-harm mentions: never treated as a violation. the post goes through as normal, and the person just sees a gentle popup with support resources alongside it.
  • reports: go through automated bots first. only what the bots can't resolve gets escalated to a human — right now, that's just the project's solo creator.
  • server hiccups: if something breaks, you're gently asked to wait a moment — never a scary error message.

open source

vent. is meant to be open-source — anyone can read the code, fork it, and fix bugs. forks are personally tested before anything gets merged.
