# vent.

A place to say whatever's on your mind.

vent. is an open platform for getting things off your chest. No filters on your feelings, no judgment on what you're going through — just a space to write and be heard. The only things not welcome here are explicit (18+) content and bullying.

## What you can do

- Create an account (username, email, password) and post whatever's on your mind.
- Vent about anything — including hard topics like stress, anxiety, or suicidal thoughts. Talking about these things is never against the rules here, and it will never get your account flagged or banned.

## Community rules

1. **No 18+ / explicit content.** Sexual or explicit material isn't allowed.
2. **No bullying.** Targeted harassment, insults, or attacks on another user are not tolerated. Venting about your own experiences — even painful ones — is not bullying and is always allowed.

## How moderation works

- **Bullying detection:** if a post is flagged as bullying, the user gets an automatic reply telling them to stop, before any further action is taken.
- **Name detector:** if a post targets another user by typing their username, that specific user won't be able to see the post — it just appears as an empty post/account to them. This shields targeted users automatically, without needing a report first.
- **Suicide / self-harm mentions:** these are never treated as violations. If a post mentions suicide or self-harm, the user simply sees a gentle popup with support resources alongside their post — no ban, no flag, no punishment.
- **Reports:** when a post is reported, it first goes through a set of automated bots for an initial pass. Only reports the bots can't resolve get escalated to a human for final review. Right now, that human is the project owner — there's no moderator team yet, though that may be added down the line.

## Reliability

If something goes wrong on the server, you won't see a scary error message. You'll just be gently asked to wait a moment and try again.

## Open source

vent. is open-source. Anyone can read the code, fork it, and contribute:

- **Bug fixes:** found something broken? Fork the repo, fix it, and submit it.
- **Review process:** all forks and contributions are personally tested by the project owner before anything gets merged. This keeps the moderation system and core rules intact even as the project grows.

## Status

This project is currently maintained solo by its creator. A moderator team may be introduced in the future as the community grows.
